# Locked Flipper card standard

Approved by teacher. Revision only; textbook reading comes first.

- Front: topic name, in textbook chapter/section order.
- Back: a clear list of generic points relevant to the topic, including definitions, uses, features, comparisons and benefits/limitations as applicable.
- Use Cambridge MS meaning as the main basis; adapt scenario wording into generic points, remove repetitions, and use supplied textbook material for missing explanations.
- No raw past-paper questions as card fronts; no yellow source-gap warnings or grading instructions in answers.
- Short, relevant examiner advice remains separate; supporting references can be expanded.
- Show individual topic frequency as a percentage. Do not combine frequencies or create priority scores.
- Retain hold/release, swipe, arrow and keyboard navigation. New topics start on their front.
